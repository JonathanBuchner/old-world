**For Use With Warhammer: The Old World Rules**

This document allows you to play a Dark Elves Army in games using the rules from Warhammer: The Old World. To use the contents of this document, you will need a copy  
of the *Warhammer: The Old World* rulebook.

The rules contained within this document allow players the opportunity to play Warhammer: The Old World using rules that we hope better reflect the legacy, history and feel of the Dark Elves faction as they have appeared in previous editions.   
These rules are intended to be fair and fun to use wherever Warhammer: The Old World is played and will be maintained in concert with the playing community for as long as it is necessary to do so.

**These rules are unofficial and in no way endorsed by the authors or publisher of Warhammer: The Old World.**

**Change Log:** 

As community feedback comes in I will update any changes I’ve made below, along with commentary for my reasoning. So far the process has been incredibly rewarding and the insights that I’ve gotten will make this a much stronger document than anything I could have done myself. So thank you very much for your input, and keep the ideas flowing\! 

Each time I make a significant update, I will also change the last number of the version. IE: Renegade Dark Elves 1.5.2.2. 

When will these be finalized? My goal is to have things finalized as soon as possible. Once I feel like we’ve ironed out the bugs and areas of contention I will begin posting the final docs on [www.squarebased.com](http://www.squarebased.com). 

Hekarti’s Blessing \- Same as High Elves (re-roll to cast 1/turn instead of 1/game.) 

* Ultimately the dispel ability does not fit the bill. Given a number of other rules are simply  the same in both factions (Sea Dragon Cloak /Lion Cloak, Elven Reflexes, Martial Prowess), it stands to reason that Hekarti and Lileath should give the same blessing too. This feels like the easiest, most defensible, and obvious change  and so I humbly submit it as the solution.  

Murderous \- Actually did change\! Had the wrong colour. 

* When I redrafted these rules I kept the original wording for Murderous (which works in all rounds of combat) and added the original Renegade change to the restriction \- allowing it to be used on all mundane weapons. Sorry for gas-lighting you. 

Will of the Gods \- Replaces Dragged Along

* Dragged along being a core rulebook rule always seemed wrong to modify. It’s also a terrible rule. I’ve added back the original rule for the Cauldron and Bloodwrack Shrine from 8th edition to both unit cards. As the Cauldron can only be ridden by a hag and is Loner, it can only ever join a unit of  Witches. But if you want to toss a Bloodwrack shrine into another unit, go  nuts I guess. 

Whip of Agony \- Remove Strikes First, Reduce cost

* Strikes First is redundant on the Whip of Agony as Beastmasters already have it. After researching previous effects for the item, decided the simplest thing to do was to reduce its cost to 20 pts. (Granted the magic whip no longer does either of the things a mundane whip does) 

Tome of Furion \- Restrict to one per model. 

* Making this common means it needs to be limited to one instance per model. 

Shield of Ghrond \- return to original cost. 

* This was an impulsive change that did not factor its effect on a common combo used with a dragon mount. Some suggested making it cav/infantry only, however, I feel that this would too strongly weaken an important option for the army. 

Cold Blooded Banner \- improve effect. 

* Change wording so that it is single use and provides a Leadership re-roll. This essentially gives a single instance of a veteran re-roll, which helps the player mitigate stupidity on Cold One Knights, rather than eliminate it if they were to have Veteran full time. A better balance I think that gives you an option rather than fully eliminating a classic downside to the unit. It also has some utility in another situation if Stupidity is not a concern. 

Focus Familiar \- Clarify dispel attempts are measured from the Familiar. 

* The ability to add 12” of range and 360 LoS to any spell is a strong one. Ruling it this way feels stylistically similar to how the game has moved with regards  to casting (Vortexes  dispelled from the vortex rather than caster).   

Cursing Word \- change to \-D3 / lasts one turn. 

* I think this is a reasonable modification between the shadow nerf of the April Legacy PDF update in 2024, and returning it to full power. Also in-line with other buff/nerf stat abilities we see elsewhere in the game. I was probably a bit impulsive putting it back to  full power, so hopefully this makes the spell useful without eliminating it from the game/making it an auto-include. . 

Assassin \- Explicitly allowed to join units with “Loner” and US 5\. 

Hydra and Kharibdyss \- points increase: Hydra 165, Kharibdyss 145\.  

* I definitely pushed the envelope with the points decrease on these monsters and I will go with the wisdom of crowds in adjusting them again.  
* Yes they’re still cheap compared to many T6 Behemoths. However these goofballs are T5 Behemoths. The only other example of a T5 Behemoth that I know of is the Mangler Squig who has significantly higher damage output potential and is at 95 points. Given these fellas are both less squishy and definitely more reliable, I feel like a  midpoint between the Mangler and  most  T6 Behemoth’s seems reasonable.   
* Yes the AP on the serrated maw has been removed from Renegades 1.0. I did this in 1.5 actually as I didn’t realize serrated maw is common to a number of creatures and always works like that.   
* As noted by many, the hydra is the mythological source material for the Regeneration rule, which famously was countered using fire. While I remain broadly annoyed that “Flammable” isn’t built into the regeneration rule in this edition, I think it’s fitting to give this to the Hydra as a niche way to counter one of its saves. I also think given that it’s the O.G regenerator, I am not too fussed that it recovers wounds on a 4+ (yes, better than Orion’s similar ability.) 

Cauldron of  Blood \- Rework. 

* In refining the Cauldron of Blood I went back to the drawing board a bit. Based on  how the Cauldron functioned in 7th edition (and using the War Altar of Sigmar / Priest of Sigmar as a proxy for it in tOW), I’ve given it another kick at the can.   
* Removed “Dragged Along. Added “Will of the Gods”  
* Increased its movement to 5   
* Rather than a bound spell (or automatic ability as it was in 7th and 8th) the effect is triggered on an unmodified LD test.   
* Reduced the Cauldron’s LD to 8 (was inexplicably 9).   
* Blessings affect the Cauldron and a single unit  within  command  range. Sisters of Slaughter cannot be selected, however,  Executioners and Assassins may be (these are the other definitely Khaine worshippers in the book).   
* Blessings cannot be combined if you have more than one Cauldron.   
* Cauldron still gives a 5++ to itself, however it only gives a 6++ to the unit it joins. Bloodshield of Khaine is again a Blessing that grants a 5++ to a unit.  
* Separated the special rules for the crew so that they don’t accidentally apply to other elements of the model. 

Black Guard of Naggarond \- Exchange Murderous for Hatred (All Enemies) 

* This restores their traditional special rule, while also maintaining consistency with Murderous being a rule for “Khainite” units.   
* Removed the 1.5 version of the halberd. The Phoenix Guard did not have their halberd upgraded to the new rules, and in the spirit of matching the Core army design philosophy, I’ve pulled this back.  


Executioners \- upped points to 14 in light of Cauldron change.

* This puts them on an even points cost with Swordmasters. YMMV as to whether this is fair or not.   
* Considered giving them Loner so that they can only be joined by the Hag. This is similar but more restrictive than  the “Warriors of the White Tower” restriction from Swordmasters. Idea was to ensure theme but this felt like too much of a nerf despite being flavourful … the tradeoff for the reward of getting some buffs from the Cauldron seemed too much. 


Shades \- Chilled out a bit. 

* Removed Veteran from special rules, and added back as an option for 1 pt / model. 

Witch Elves \- Removed Elven Reflexes (redundant with Strike First) 

Sisters of Slaughter \- Added first to the fray

* Added the special rule from Dwarf Deathseekers to give them the baby version of swiftstride .  Overall seem like a more interesting unit than they were, but probably still not setting the world on fire. 

Cold One Knights \- Removed Veteran Option 

* As noted by many \- Stupidity is a core part of their identity and is mitigated well by high leadership in the army. Cold One Banner was also upgraded in the event that someone would still like to have a reliable “get out of stupid” card. 

Scourge Runner Chariots \- Removed ponderous on the Ravager Harpoon. 

Cold One Chariots \- Small points reduction. (currently pointed equal to Lion Chariot, arguably worse) 

Stoney Stare (Medusae shooting attack) \- Added multiple shots D3. In the past this was a multiple shots 4 shooting attack with killing blow, so feels like a reasonable tOW translation. The Shrine also does D3 of a similar attack to each model in base that fails an initiative test so seemed to jive well. 

Bloodwrack Shrine \- It’s a big ol expensive chariot I guess\! 

* For lack of better alternatives, mostly just kept it as is except gave it the updated “Will of the Gods” rule. Not every model has to be a winner.

# **Dark Elves Legacy Army List** {#dark-elves-legacy-army-list}

This army list is intended for use alongside the ‘Forming Units’ and ‘Warhammer Armies’ sections of the *Warhammer: the Old World* rulebook. Over the following pages you will find profiles and rules for each of the models in your army. These pages give you all of the information that you need to shape your collection of models into the units which will in turn form a force ready for battle.

## **Grand Army Composition List** {#grand-army-composition-list}

**Characters**

Up to 50% of your army’s points value may be spent on:

* 0-1 Dark Elf Dreadlord or Supreme Sorceress per 1,000 points  
* Dark Elf Masters, Sorceresses, High Beastmasters, Death Hags and Khainite Assassins  
* 0-1 Cauldron of Blood per 1,000 points (taken as a mount for a Death Hag)

**Core**

At least 25% of your army’s points value must be spent on:

* Dark Elf Warriors, Repeater Crossbowmen, Black Ark Corsairs and Dark Riders  
* If your army includes one or more Death Hags, 0-1 unit of Witch Elves may be taken as a Core choice

**Special**

Up to 50% of your army’s points value may be spent on:

* Har Ganeth Executioners, Witch Elves, Dark Elf Shades and Harpies  
* 0-1 unit of Black Guard of Naggarond per 1,000 points  
* 0-1 unit of Cold One Knights per 1,000 points

* 0-2 Scourgerunner Chariots or Cold One Chariots per 1,000 points  
* If your army includes a High Beastmaster, 0-1 War Hydra or Kharibdyss may be taken as a Special choice

**Rare**

Up to 25% of your army’s points value may be spent on:

* Sisters of Slaughter, Bloodwrack Shrines, War Hydras, Kharibdyss, Merwyrms *(See page 45 of Arcane Journal: High Elves)* and Bloodwrack Medusas  
* 0-1 unit of Doomfire Warlocks per 1,000 points

* 0-2 Reaper Bolt Throwers per 1,000 points

**Mercenaries**

Up to 20% of your army’s points value may be spent on mercenaries.

**Allies**

Up to 25% of your army’s points value may be spent on a single allied contingent drawn from one of the following Grand Army composition lists:

* Warriors of Chaos (Uneasy)

**Battle Standard Bearer**

A single Dark Elf Master in your army may be upgraded to be your Battle Standard Bearer for \+25 points. In addition to their usual allowance of points to spend on magic items, a Battle Standard Bearer can purchase a single magic standard with no points limit.

**Dark Elves Special Rules**

**game of Warhammer: the Old World is packed with fantastic creatures and skilled warriors with abilities so incredible and varied that the basic rules cannot possibly cover them all. For such circumstances we have special rules – uncommon rules to govern uncommon circumstances.**

On this page you will find a full description for each of the Army special rules used by models drawn from the Dark Elves army list:

**Cleaving Blow**  
If a model with this special rule rolls a natural 6 when making a roll To Wound for an attack made in combat, it has struck a ‘Cleaving Blow.’ Enemy models whose troop type is ‘regular infantry,’ ‘heavy infantry,’ ‘light cavalry,’ ‘heavy cavalry’ or ‘war beasts’ are not permitted an armour or Regeneration save against a Cleaving Blow (Ward saves can be attempted as normal).

*Note that if an attack wounds automatically, this special rule cannot be used.*

**Elven Reflexes**  
A model with this special rule (but not its mount) has a \+1 modifier to its Initiative characteristic (to a maximum of 10\) during the first round of any combat.

**Eternal Hatred**

Against High Elves, this model’s Hatred applies in every round of close combat, not just the first.

*Note that this special rule does not apply to this model’s mount (should it have one).*

**Will of the Gods**   
A model with this special rule may join a unit of Infantry with unit strength 10 or more despite being a Heavy Chariot.  If it does so, it must always be placed in the centre of the front rank, and it no longer has the Lumbering rule (Rulebook p. 195). Only one model with this special rule may join each unit.   **Hekarti’s Blessing**  
Once per turn, a model with this special rule may re-roll a single failed Casting roll.

**Martial Prowess**  
A unit with this special rule can make supporting attacks to its flank or rear, as well as to its front.

**Murderous**  
When engaged in combat, a model with this special rule may re-roll any rolls To Wound of a natural 1\.

*Note that this special rule only applies to mundane weapons and does not apply to a model’s mount (should it have one). If the model is using a magic weapon this special rule ceases to apply.*

**Sea Dragon Cloak**

A model with this special rule improves its armour value by 1 (to a maximum of 2+) against non-magical shooting attacks.

**Arcane Items**

**Black Staff	55 points**

The bearer of the Black Staff may use it when attempting to cast a spell. If they do so, roll an extra D6 when making the Casting roll and discard the lowest result. However, if a double 1 is rolled on any two of the dice rolled, the spell is miscast.

**Focus Familiar\*	10 points**

Single use. The owner of a Focus Familiar may use it when they attempt to cast a spell. Place a marker (such as a Familiar model) completely within 12" of the owner. The range (including the range to dispel unless it is a vortex) and all effects of the spell, are measured from this marker, rather than from the owner. If the spell requires a line of sight, this is determined from the marker (which has a 360° vision arc). If the spell remains in play, keep the marker on the board until the spell is dispelled or removed from play.  

**Tome Of Furion\*	15 points**

0-1 per model. The bearer of the Tome of Furion knows one more spell (chosen in the usual way) than is normal for their Level of Wizardry.

**Hidden**  
Khainite Assassins are not placed on the battlefield at the start of the game. Instead, they are ‘hidden’ within a friendly Dark Elf unit whose troop type is ‘infantry’ and that has a Unit Strength of five or more (excluding Harpies, including units with the Loner special rule). Make a note of which unit each Assassin is hiding within. A hidden Assassin may be revealed during any Start of Turn sub-phase or at the start of any Combat phase. Position the revealed Assassin as you would a character that has joined the unit.

If a unit in which a Khainite Assassin is hiding is destroyed or flees the battlefield before the Assassin is revealed, the Assassin is removed as a casualty. A Khainite Assassin cannot be your army General.

* **Manbane:** When this character makes a roll To Wound, a roll of 4+ is always a success, regardless of the target’s Toughness.
