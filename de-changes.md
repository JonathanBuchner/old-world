## Dark Elf Special Rules

### Eternal Hatred (X)
+ Change to Eternal Hatred (X)
Against the unit listed in parentheses, this model’s Hatred applies in every round of close combat, not just the first.

### Hatred Dark Elves
+ When playing High Elves, any High Elf model with Elven Reflexes gains the **Hatred (Dark Elves)** special rule.  In addition, Characters gain **Eternal Hatred (Dark Elves)**. Hatred (Dark Elves) does not apply to mounts.

+ ### Sea Skin (New Rule)
+ A model with this special rule improves its armour value by 1 (to a maximum of 2+) against shooting attacks and has 
*Hydra and Karibdyss need some additional protection to get through the shooting phase.*
